Ration Card Services

📌 Service Introduction

A ration card is an essential document issued by the State Food and Civil Supplies Department. It provides subsidized food grains (wheat, rice, sugar, etc.) to eligible families and serves as a valid proof of identity and address. Get information regarding new ration cards (APL/BPL/Antyodaya), adding/deleting names, and checking status from here.

📋 Required Documents

  • Head of Family's Identity Proof: [Aadhaar Redacted] Card, Photo, and Bank Passbook details.
  • Documents of All Members: [Aadhaar Redacted] Cards of all family members.
  • Proof of Address: Electricity Bill, Water Bill, or Income Certificate.
  • Others: Family passport size group photo and active mobile number.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. What is 'One Nation One Ration Card' (ONORC)?

Ans: Under this scheme, you can get food grains from any government ration shop in any state/city of the country using your existing ration card.

Q2. How to check your name in the ration card list?

Ans: You can view the list by visiting the NFSA portal or the official website of your state's food department and selecting your district and block/gram panchayat.
