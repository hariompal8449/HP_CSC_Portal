Scholarship Portal

📌 Service Introduction

Scholarships are provided by Central and State Governments to meritorious students and economically weaker sections to continue their education. Through this portal, complete processes and links for fresh and renewal scholarship applications for Pre-Matric (9th-10th), Post-Matric (11th-12th), and Higher Education (UG/PG/Diploma) are available.

📋 Required Documents

  • Academic Documents: Previous class Mark Sheet and Fee Receipt.
  • Identity & Residence: Student's Aadhaar Card and Domicile Certificate.
  • Income & Caste Certificate: Father/Guardian's Income Certificate and Caste Certificate.
  • Bank Details: Student's Aadhaar-seeded NPCI linked bank account.
  • Others: Student's passport size photo and active mobile number.

⏱️ Time & Fee

⏱️ Application Timeline:

As per academic session (Generally July to December)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. In which account is the scholarship money credited directly?

Ans: Through DBT, scholarship payments are sent to the student's bank account that is Aadhaar-seeded (NPCI Linked).

Q2. Do renewal students need to do a new registration?

Ans: No, by using the previous year's registration number and password, you can directly go to 'Renewal Login' and update your form.
