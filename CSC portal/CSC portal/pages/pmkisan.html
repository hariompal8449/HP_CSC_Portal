PM-KISAN Scheme

📌 Service Introduction

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a flagship scheme of the Government of India under which financial assistance of ₹6,000 per year is provided to eligible farmer families. This amount is directly transferred into the bank accounts of farmers (DBT) in three equal installments of ₹2,000. From here, check new farmer registration, OTP/Face e-KYC, and installment status (Beneficiary Status).

📋 Required Documents

  • Applicant's Identity Proof: [Aadhaar Redacted].
  • Land Documents: Khatauni / Jamabandi (For Land Seeding).
  • Bank Account Details: Bank Passbook (Mandatory link with [Aadhaar Redacted]).
  • Others: Active Mobile Number (For OTP Verification).

⏱️ Benefits & Assistance Amount

💰 Annual Financial Assistance:

₹6,000 per year (₹2,000 x 3 installments)

💰 Portal/CSC Fee:

Online Application / e-KYC: ₹15 - ₹50

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. What is required to receive the next installment?

Ans: To receive installments, it is mandatory to complete e-KYC, have Land Seeding status as 'YES', and have the bank account seeded with [Aadhaar Redacted].

Q2. How can I do PM-KISAN e-KYC?

Ans: You can do free self e-KYC on the portal using [Aadhaar Redacted] OTP or visit the nearest CSC center for biometric/face authentication.
