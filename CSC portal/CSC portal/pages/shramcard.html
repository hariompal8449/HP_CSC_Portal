Labour / e-Shram Card Services

Service Introduction

The e-Shram portal is operated by the Government of India to create a national database of unorganized workers and provide them with the benefits of government schemes.

Required Documents

  • Identity Proof: [Aadhaar Redacted]
  • Mobile Number: Mobile number linked with Aadhaar card (For OTP verification)
  • Bank Details: Bank passbook (Account number and IFSC code)
  • Other Details: Age limit 16 to 59 years, Occupation details

Time & Fee

⏱️ Estimated Time: Instant (Tatkal Registration)

💰 Government/CSC Fee: Registration on the portal is completely free. Printing charges may apply at CSC centers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Who is eligible to get an e-Shram card?
Ans: All unorganized sector workers (such as laborers, cart drivers, domestic workers, etc.) aged between 16 and 59 years are eligible.
Q2. Is there any fee for this?
Ans: No, registration on the e-Shram portal is completely free of cost.
