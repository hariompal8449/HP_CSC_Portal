PAN Card Services

📌 Service Introduction

A PAN (Permanent Account Number) card is a 10-digit alphanumeric number issued by the Income Tax Department. It is mandatory for financial transactions, opening bank accounts, and filing taxes. Through this page, understand the complete process for a new PAN card (Form 49A), corrections, e-PAN download, and PAN-Aadhaar linking.

📋 Required Documents

  • Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Driving License.
  • Proof of Address: Aadhaar Card, Electricity Bill, or Bank Passbook.
  • Proof of Date of Birth: Aadhaar Card, Birth Certificate, or 10th Class Marksheet.
  • Others: 2 Passport Size Color Photos and Active Mobile Number.

⏱️ Time & Fee

⏱️ Estimated Time:

e-PAN: 2 to 3 Days | Physical Card: 10 to 15 Days

💰 Government/CSC Fee:

For Indian Address: ₹107 (Including GST)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Can an instant PAN card be made using Aadhaar?

Ans: Yes, if your mobile number is linked with Aadhaar, an instant e-PAN can be downloaded for free from the Income Tax e-filing portal.

Q2. How to correct name or date of birth in PAN card?

Ans: You can apply for corrections online or through a CSC center with proper documents using the 'PAN Correction Form'.
