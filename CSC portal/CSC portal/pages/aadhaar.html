Aadhaar Card Services

📌 Service Introduction

Aadhaar is a 12-digit identification number issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI). Through this portal, you can find information regarding new Aadhaar enrollment, name/address/mobile updates, e-Aadhaar download, Aadhaar PVC card ordering, and tracking status.

📋 Required Documents

  • Proof of Identity (PoI): Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport, or Driving License.
  • Proof of Address (PoA): Ration Card, Electricity Bill, Water Bill, or Bank Passbook.
  • Proof of Date of Birth (DoB): Birth Certificate or 10th Class Marksheet.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Can mobile number be updated in Aadhaar online?

Ans: To update your mobile number, you must visit a nearby CSC Center or Aadhaar Seva Kendra for biometric verification.

Q2. How to download e-Aadhaar?

Ans: Go to the myAadhaar portal, select the 'Download Aadhaar' option, enter your details, and download using the OTP received on your registered mobile number.
