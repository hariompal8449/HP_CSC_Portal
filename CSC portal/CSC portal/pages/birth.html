Birth Certificate

📌 Service Introduction

A birth certificate is a vital legal document issued by the state government, municipal corporation, or gram panchayat. It serves as an official proof of a child's birth, date, and place. It is mandatory for school admissions, passports, identity cards, and all other governmental works.

📋 Required Documents

  • Parents' Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card or Voter ID.
  • Birth Receipt / Hospital Discharge Slip: Certificate issued by the hospital.
  • Proof of Address: Electricity Bill, Ration Card, or Landlord's Consent Letter.
  • In Case of Delay: Affidavit and permission from the SDM/Tehsildar.

⏱️ Time & Fee

⏱️ Estimated Time:

7 to 21 Working Days (After CRS Portal / Municipal verification)

💰 Government/CSC Fee:

Within 21 days: Free | After 21 days: Late fee (₹10 - ₹50)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Within how many days of birth is registration free?

Ans: Registration within 21 days of birth is completely free of cost.

Q2. Can a birth certificate be made for older births (more than 1 year)?

Ans: Yes, delayed registration can be done through an order from the SDM or First Class Magistrate along with an affidavit.
