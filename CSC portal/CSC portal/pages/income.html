Income Certificate

📌 Service Introduction

An income certificate is an official document issued by the state government that certifies the annual income of an individual or their family from all sources. It is required to avail scholarships, government schemes, and fee concessions in educational institutions.

📋 Required Documents

  • Applicant's Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or PAN Card.
  • Self-Declaration Form: Signed by the applicant.
  • Proof of Income: Salary Slip, Patwari Report, or Bank Passbook.
  • Others: 1 Passport Size Photo and Ration Card.

Q1. What is the validity of an income certificate?

Ans: An income certificate is valid for 3 years from the date of issue.
