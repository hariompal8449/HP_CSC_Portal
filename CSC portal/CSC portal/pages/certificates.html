Certificate Services

Income, Caste & Domicile Certificates

Various certificate services available on the e-Sathi portal

Certificate Overview

  • Income Certificate: Used to certify annual family income for scholarships, government schemes, and financial aid (Validity: 3 Years).
  • Caste Certificate: For SC, ST, and OBC citizens to avail reservation and scheme benefits (Validity: Lifetime).
  • Domicile Certificate: Proof of being a permanent resident of the state, required for local jobs and documents (Validity: Lifetime).

Time & Fee

⏱️ Estimated Time: 7 to 10 Working Days

💰 Government/CSC Fee: Approximately ₹20 to ₹30 per certificate

Required Documents

  • Identity Proof: [Aadhaar Redacted] / PAN Card / Voter ID
  • Self-Declaration Form (Signed or thumb impression)
  • Ration Card or Electricity Bill (for Domicile), Income Details (for Income)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. How long is an income certificate valid?
Ans: An income certificate remains valid for 3 years from the date of issue, whereas caste and domicile certificates are valid for a lifetime.
