Caste Certificate

📌 Service Introduction

A caste certificate is an important document issued by the state government, certifying that the applicant belongs to a specific caste or community (OBC, SC, ST, EWS). It is used to avail reservation in government jobs, scholarships, and concessions in educational institutions.

📋 Required Documents

  • Proof of Identity: Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or PAN Card.
  • Proof of Address: Electricity Bill, Ration Card, or Domicile Certificate.
  • Proof of Caste: Father's/Grandfather's Caste Certificate, Land Khatauni, or Old Marksheet.
  • Others: Self-Declaration Form and 1 Passport Size Photo.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. What is the validity of a caste certificate?

Ans: For SC/ST it is valid for a lifetime, whereas for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) and EWS, it needs to be updated annually or every 3 years depending on state guidelines.

Q2. Can a caste certificate be applied without father's certificate?

Ans: It is generally required to attach a paternal relative's caste certificate or revenue/patwari report for verification.
