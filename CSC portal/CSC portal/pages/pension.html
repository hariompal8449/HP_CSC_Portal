Pension Schemes

📌 Service Introduction

Various pension schemes are run by the Social Welfare Department to provide financial assistance to senior citizens, widows, and persons with disabilities. Through this portal, get information regarding new applications, status checks, and e-KYC for Old Age Pension, Widow Pension, Disability Pension, and Atal Pension Yojana (APY).

📋 Required Documents

  • Proof of Identity: Aadhaar Card or Voter ID Card.
  • Proof of Income: Income Certificate (below poverty line or within specified limits).
  • Bank Account Details: Copy of Bank Passbook (Aadhaar seeding mandatory).
  • Other Specific Documents: Age proof (for old age), Husband's death certificate (for widow pension), or 40%+ disability certificate (for disability pension).

⏱️ Time & Fee

⏱️ Estimated Time:

30 to 45 Working Days (After verification)

💰 Government/CSC Fee:

Online Application: Free | CSC Service Fee: ₹30 - ₹50

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. What to do if pension money is not credited to the account?

Ans: Link your bank account with Aadhaar (Aadhaar Seeding) and complete your Aadhaar e-KYC process on the portal.

Q2. What is the minimum age limit for old age pension?

Ans: The applicant must be 60 years of age or older to apply for the old age pension scheme.
