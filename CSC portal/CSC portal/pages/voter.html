Voter ID Services

📌 Service Introduction

Voter ID Card is an official document issued by the Election Commission of India (ECI). It grants citizens of the country the right to vote and serves as a valid proof of identity and address. Understand the procedure for new voter card (Form 6), correction (Form 8), digital e-EPIC download, and searching name in the electoral roll from here.

📋 Required Documents

  • Proof of Age: Aadhaar Card, 10th Class Marksheet, PAN Card, or Birth Certificate (18 years or above).
  • Proof of Address: Aadhaar Card, Electricity/Water Bill, Bank Passbook, or Ration Card.
  • Others: 1 Passport size photo and active mobile number.

⏱️ Time & Fee

⏱️ Estimated Time:

15 to 30 Working Days (After BLO verification)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. What is the minimum age to get a voter card?

Ans: The minimum age to get a voter identity card in India should be 18 years (as per the qualifying date).

Q2. Can I download the digital voter card (e-EPIC)?

Ans: Yes, if your mobile number is registered on the voter portal, you can download the e-EPIC in PDF format.
